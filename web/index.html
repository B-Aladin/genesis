            How to capture good evidence
            1. Photos straight from above, in good light, every marking readable. Fill the frame with the board.
            2. Photograph the back too: the copper is the only way to trace the wiring.
            3. Video 10 to 20 seconds with the phone resting on something, the device running, no zooming.
            4. iPhone: Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible, so the video is MP4 (H.264).
            5. Say what you know in the note (battery voltage, what it does) and tick the tools you have.
            2 What you have on hand optional · next steps use these
            3 What you already know optional · treated as a hint, not evidence

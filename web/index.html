      How to capture good evidence
      1. Straight from above, good light, every marking readable. Open the case if you safely can.
      2. Photograph the back: the copper is the only way to trace the wiring.
      3. Video 10–20 s, phone resting still, the device running.
      4. iPhone: Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible (MP4).
      5. Mains devices: unplug and wait before opening; capacitors can stay charged.
